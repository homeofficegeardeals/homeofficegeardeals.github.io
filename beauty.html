Frequently Asked Questions

How often do heads and blades need replacing?

Electric toothbrush heads are usually changed every three months; trimmer blades last longer but cost more when they go.

Will a US plug work abroad?

Check the charger input range. Many are 100 to 240 V, but the plug shape still differs.

Does waterproofing matter?

For bathroom use, yes. Look for a stated water-resistance rating rather than a marketing word.
