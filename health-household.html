Air quality, first aid and hydration gear. We pick on filter cost, room size and noise — and we make no medical claims about any of it.

What to check before you buy

  • Room size: match the unit to the listed coverage, not to the photo
  • Filter cost and availability: the yearly cost is the filters, not the unit
  • Noise at the setting you will actually leave it on overnight
  • Whether it needs an app or an account before it will run
  • Thermometer type: contact versus infrared suit different jobs
  • Consumables: pads, filters and refills are recurring spend
