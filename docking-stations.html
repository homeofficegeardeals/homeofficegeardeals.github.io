Frequently Asked Questions

Will a dock work with my laptop?

Check the USB-C or Thunderbolt version and whether the laptop supports video over USB-C. A dock cannot add a feature the port does not have.

How much power should the dock deliver?

Match the laptop's charger wattage. An underpowered dock will charge slowly or not at all while driving external screens.

Can one dock drive two monitors?

Only if the listing states dual display support and your laptop allows it. Some USB-C ports split bandwidth and limit the second screen.
