What actually decides it

Marketing copy hides the trade-offs in the same three places on almost every desk accessorie. These are the ones worth checking against your own setup before you order.

  • The problem it solves: the first number that can rule the product out. Measure it against your own setup rather than the photos, because most returns start here.
  • Desk space used versus freed: where cheaper versions usually cut corners. It is rarely in the headline spec.
  • Compatibility (VESA, OS, ports): the compatibility detail that is easy to miss on the listing and expensive to discover afterwards.
  • Build quality and warranty: the part that decides the cost over a year, not on day one. Consumables and replacement parts matter more than the sticker.

What the listing leaves out

Product pages rarely mention return shipping, the exact size with packaging, or which parts the warranty excludes.
