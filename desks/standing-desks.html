Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a dual motor desk?

For a 48×24 top at this price, yes — all three picks use dual motors, which keeps the rise even and reduces wobble at standing height. Single-motor frames appear mostly below this size.

How much weight can the EN1 hold?

Flexispot rates the EN1 at 176 lb. Two monitors, a desktop tower and a printer sit comfortably inside that. The FEZIBO listings do not publish a load rating, so treat the EN1 as the safer pick for a heavy setup.

Is the one-piece top worth paying for?

If you clamp a monitor arm or a mic boom to the desk, yes: a three-piece top has a seam that a clamp can straddle. For a plain keyboard-and-laptop setup the difference is cosmetic.
