❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I stand vs. sit each day?

Start with the 50/30/20 rule: 50% sitting, 30% standing, 20% walking or stretching every hour. Gradually increase standing time by 10–15 minutes per week until you're comfortable with 4–6 hours standing daily.

Are these desks good for people under 5' tall?

The Flexispot E7 and Uplift V2 both accommodate shorter users with their wide height ranges (25.3"–51"). The FEZIBO works best for average heights but may need desk risers for users under 4'10".

Can I convert this to a traditional sitting desk?

Yes! All top picks feature manual lowering buttons or controllers with "sit-down" presets. Simply lower the desk below your preferred sitting height and secure it using the controller's locking mechanism.

What's included in the assembly kit?

All desks include: power cable, control panel, mounting brackets, screws and bolts, Allen wrenches, and step-by-step instructions. The Flexispot E7 also includes a cable management tray under the desktop.
