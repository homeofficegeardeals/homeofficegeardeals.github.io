Clothing & Accessories

Bags, socks and basics for the commute. Checked on internal dimensions and fabric blend, because those decide whether it works.

What to check before you buy

  • Laptop sleeve size: a 16-inch sleeve is not a 16-inch laptop, measure diagonally
  • Fabric blend: the percentage of merino or cotton changes everything
  • Strap and hardware: the parts that fail before the fabric does
  • Size chart against a garment you already own, not the model photo
  • Care instructions: anything that needs hand washing gets worn less
  • Return window, because sizing is the main return risk
