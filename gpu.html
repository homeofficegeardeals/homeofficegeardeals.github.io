Frequently Asked Questions

How much VRAM do I need?

8 GB for 1080p gaming, 12 to 16 GB for 1440p and creative work. Local AI models want as much as you can afford.

Will my power supply cope?

Check the card's recommended PSU wattage and the number of power connectors. A quality 650 W unit covers most mid-range cards.

Will it fit my case?

Measure the clearance against the card length in the listing. Triple-fan cards are often 30 cm or longer.
