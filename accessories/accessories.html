The desk upgrades that earn their space: mice, monitor arms, stands and the cable fixes that cost less than a monitor.

What to check before you buy

  • The problem it solves, before the feature list
  • Desk space it takes versus the space it frees
  • Compatibility: VESA pattern, operating system, port types
  • Build quality and warranty, especially on moving parts
  • Consumables and replacement cost over a year
  • Cable routing, or you will be untangling it monthly

Frequently Asked Questions

Which ergonomic mouse is best for beginners?

The Logitech MX Master 3S vertical mouse is perfect for beginners. Its ergonomic design prevents wrist strain while maintaining familiar button placement and cursor precision.

How do I mount a monitor arm?

Most arms install with standard VESA screw holes. Use the included drill bit to create mounting holes in your desk. Attach the arm bracket first, then clamp it to the desktop. Position monitors according to your preferred viewing angle.

Can I use these accessories with a standing desk?

Yes! All listed accessories work perfectly with electric and manual standing desks. The monitor arms adjust as you change desk height, and vertical mice remain comfortable in both sitting and standing positions.
