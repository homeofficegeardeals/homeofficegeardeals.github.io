Frequently Asked Questions

Mesh or a single router?

One router is enough for a flat under about 90 square metres. Mesh helps when walls or floors block a single unit.

Do I need Wi-Fi 7?

Not yet for most homes. Wi-Fi 6 handles video calls and streaming comfortably; Wi-Fi 7 matters if you move large files between devices.

Where should the router sit?

Central, off the floor and away from metal and microwaves. Placement usually beats a more expensive model.
