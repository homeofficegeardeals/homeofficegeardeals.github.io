Home Storage & Cleaning

Storage that fits the shelf you actually have, and cleaning gear where the running cost matters more than the sticker.

What to check before you buy

  • Measure the gap, not the product photo: shelves are usually 30–35 cm deep
  • Open versus closed storage: dust is the hidden cost of open shelving
  • Bags versus bagless: bags cost money forever, bagless costs filters and cleaning time
  • Battery runtime and charge time, both listed, not just one
  • App or account requirements: some robots refuse to run without an app login
