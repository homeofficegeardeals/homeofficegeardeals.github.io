Frequently Asked Questions

Which webcam is best for low-light rooms?

The Logitech Brio 4K with HDR support performs best in challenging lighting. The C920s Pro also handles dim light well but may need additional room lighting for optimal results.

Do these work on MacBooks?

Yes! All Logitech webcams are plug-and-play on macOS. Simply connect via USB and they'll appear instantly in Camera, Zoom, Teams, or OBS without additional drivers.

Can I use these for Twitch streaming?

The Logitech Brio 4K is excellent for streaming with its 60fps capability and HDR. The C920s Pro works well for beginners, while the Elgato Facecam is preferred by professional streamers.

What's the best mounting option?

All webcams include universal monitor clips. For laptop use, consider a flexible gooseneck mount ($15–$25). Tripod compatibility is built into all models with standard 1/4" thread.
