Frequently Asked Questions

SSD or hard drive?

SSD for the system and anything you use daily. A hard drive is still cheaper per terabyte for backups and media archives.

NVMe or SATA?

NVMe is faster and now similar in price for 1 TB. Check the motherboard has a free M.2 slot and the right key type.

How long will an SSD last?

Look at the endurance rating in terabytes written. Normal desktop use rarely approaches it, but heavy video work does.
