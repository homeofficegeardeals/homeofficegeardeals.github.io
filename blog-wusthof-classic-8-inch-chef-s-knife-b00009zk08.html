What actually decides it

Marketing copy hides the trade-offs in the same three places on almost every cookware. These are the ones worth checking against your own setup before you order.

  • Magnetic base for induction: the first number that can rule the product out. Measure it against your own setup rather than the photos, because most returns start here.
  • Dishwasher limits: where cheaper versions usually cut corners. It is rarely in the headline spec.
  • Weight and handle: the compatibility detail that is easy to miss on the listing and expensive to discover afterwards.
  • Lid fit: the part that decides the cost over a year, not on day one. Consumables and replacement parts matter more than the sticker.

What the listing leaves out

Product pages rarely mention return shipping, the exact size with packaging, or which parts the warranty excludes. For a cookware, the three omissions that matter most are magnetic base for induction, dishwasher limits and lid fit. Check the specification table and the seller's returns page before ordering, not after.
