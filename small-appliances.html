Frequently Asked Questions

How much counter space will it take?

Measure the footprint, not just the capacity. Air fryers and coffee machines are usually deeper than they look in photos.

Are the parts dishwasher safe?

Check each removable part. Baskets and trays often are; heating elements and bases never are.

Do filters need replacing?

Coffee machines and air fryers with filters have a replacement interval. Factor that running cost in before buying.
