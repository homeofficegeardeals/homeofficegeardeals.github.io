Frequently Asked Questions

How long does assembly take?

All top picks assemble in 20–45 minutes with included tools and clear instructions. The process is straightforward if you clear a flat workspace first. Video tutorials available on Amazon product pages.

Can I use these chairs with dual monitors?

Yes! All ergonomic chairs have adjustable arms and swivel bases designed to accommodate dual monitor setups up to 40 inches combined. The Herman Miller Aeron and Steelcase Leap even offer optional sit-stand compatibility.

What's the return policy on Amazon?

Amazon offers free returns within 30 days of purchase for most office furniture. Check the specific product page for exact terms. Some sellers offer 90-day or longer warranties beyond Amazon's standard return window.

Are these chairs good for people over 250 lbs?

The Herman Miller Aeron supports up to 350 lbs, making it the top choice for heavier users. The Steelcase Leap V2 supports up to 400 lbs. Check individual product pages for exact weight capacity and consider contacting sellers about extended warranties.
