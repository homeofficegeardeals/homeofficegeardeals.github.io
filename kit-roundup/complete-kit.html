Complete Setup Guide: How to Arrange Your Home Office

Step 1: Assemble Standing Desk First (if applicable)

Time: 45–60 minutes | People Needed: 2 recommended for electric desks

  1. Lay out all parts and verify nothing is damaged
  2. Attach monitor arm bracket to underside of desktop before assembly
  3. Install legs with anti-wobble design (tighten all bolts firmly)
  4. Test lift mechanism at minimum 4 different heights
  5. Set memory presets: sitting height, standing height, and middle position

Step 2: Install Monitor Arm (if applicable)

Time: 20–30 minutes | People Needed: 1

  1. Attach arm brackets to underside of desktop at preferred positions
  2. Mount monitors with VESA screw holes (standard size: 75×75mm or 100×100mm)
  3. Position screens at eye level when seated (top of monitor at/below eye level)
  4. Use cable clips to hide power and HDMI cables underneath desk

Step 3: Assemble Ergonomic Chair

Time: 20–30 minutes | People Needed: 1

  1. Lay out all parts and verify nothing is missing or damaged
  2. Attach base plate to seat cushion with included screws
  3. Screw star base onto bottom of seat (tighten evenly in circular motion)
  4. Attach gas lift cylinder between seat and star base
  5. Mount backrest and headrest if applicable
  6. Test all adjustment features before use

Step 4: Connect Webcam and Headphones

Time: 5–10 minutes | People Needed: 1

  1. Connect webcam to monitor or laptop via USB (preferably near top of screen)
  2. Test webcam in video call software before starting work
  3. Pair wireless headphones with computer via Bluetooth pairing process
  4. Position headphones for optimal microphone pickup (near mouth level)

Step 5: Install Cable Management System

Time: 15–20 minutes | People Needed: 1

  1. Run power cables through under-desk cable management channel
  2. Attach cable clips to monitor arms for wire organization
  3. Use velcro ties to bundle cables together (don't overtighten)
  4. Hide excess cable length under desk or along baseboard
  5. Test all devices to ensure proper function after cabling

Step 6: Position Desk Mat Bundle

Time: 2–5 minutes | People Needed: 1

  1. Clean desk surface and place mat in center of workspace
  2. Position keyboard on left side, mouse on right side (for right-handed users)
  3. Align wireless charging pad with phone placement preferences
  4. Test mouse tracking across entire mat surface for smooth operation

Ergonomics Tips for Complete Setup

  • Chair Height: Adjust so feet rest flat on floor with 90° knee angle. Feet should be at or below heart level when seated.
  • Monitor Position: Top of monitor should be at or slightly below eye level (prevent neck strain from looking up)
  • Armrest Height: Elbows should rest comfortably at 90° angle with forearms parallel to floor
  • Desk Height: Elbows should be close to body with shoulders relaxed (not hunched forward)
  • Laptop Position: Keep laptop on monitor arm or external keyboard/mouse for better ergonomics
