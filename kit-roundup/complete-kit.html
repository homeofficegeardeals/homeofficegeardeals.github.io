Four pieces that decide how a home office feels: chair, desk, webcam, headphones. Buy in any order — the picks work on their own.

If You Only Buy One Thing

Back pain first

Start with the chair. A desk does not fix posture on its own, and a mesh seat with adjustable lumbar support is the change most people feel within a week. See the ergonomic chairs page for cheaper alternatives.

Calls first

Start with the webcam and headphones. Built-in laptop cameras look worse than a 1080p webcam, and a noisy room is what people on the call actually notice. Compare the webcams and headphones pages.

Standing desk first

If you already have a decent chair, the desk is the bigger change. The standing desks page compares three 48×24 electric frames on presets, warranty and whether the top is one piece.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to buy all four?

No. Each pick is independent and none of them needs the others to work. The order above is the one most people notice first, not a requirement.

Will these fit a small room?

The desk is 48×24, which is the usual small-room size: a monitor, keyboard and laptop fit without taking over the floor. The Aeron is a standard-size task chair rather than an executive recliner.

Why no total price?

Because prices move weekly and a quoted total would be wrong within days. Each card links to the live Amazon price instead.
