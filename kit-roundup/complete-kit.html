Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to buy all four?

No. Each pick is independent and none of them needs the others to work. The order above is the one most people notice first, not a requirement.

Will these fit a small room?

The desk is 48×24, which is the usual small-room size: a monitor, keyboard and laptop fit without taking over the floor. The Aeron is a standard-size task chair rather than an executive recliner.

Why no total price?

Because prices move weekly and a quoted total would be wrong within days. Each card links to the live Amazon price instead.
