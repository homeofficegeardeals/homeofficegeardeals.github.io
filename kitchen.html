Kitchen Cookware & Knives

Pots, pans and knives that survive daily cooking. The picks below are chosen for heat compatibility and how long they last, not for a brand badge.

What to check before you buy

  • Induction compatibility: a pan that only works on gas is a problem if you move flat
  • Pan body versus coating: an uncoated steel or cast pan outlives any non-stick layer
  • Knife steel and geometry: a thin blade that flexes is not a chef's knife
  • Handle and weight: a full pan is heavier than the one in the shop
  • Dishwasher claims: most non-stick and most wooden handles do not survive one

Frequently Asked Questions

Will this pan work on induction?

Only if the listing says induction-compatible or the base is magnetic. A fridge magnet is a quick test.

Is it dishwasher safe?

Check the manufacturer's note. Knives and cast iron generally are not, even when the pan itself is.

How should I care for a knife?

Hand wash, dry immediately and store it off the blade edge. A honing rod maintains the edge; a sharpener removes metal.
