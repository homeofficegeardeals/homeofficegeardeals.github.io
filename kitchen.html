Frequently Asked Questions

Will this pan work on induction?

Only if the listing says induction-compatible or the base is magnetic. A fridge magnet is a quick test.

Is it dishwasher safe?

Check the manufacturer's note. Knives and cast iron generally are not, even when the pan itself is.

How should I care for a knife?

Hand wash, dry immediately and store it off the blade edge. A honing rod maintains the edge; a sharpener removes metal.
