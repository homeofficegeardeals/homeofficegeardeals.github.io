Frequently Asked Questions

Is a faster processor worth it over more RAM?

For everyday work, more RAM and a better screen matter more than the top processor tier. Only heavy compiling or editing changes that order.

How do I know if the RAM is soldered?

The listing usually says onboard or soldered. If it does not, assume it cannot be upgraded and pick the capacity you will need in two years.

Do I need a docking station?

If you use an external monitor, keyboard and mouse, a single USB-C dock is simpler than plugging in four cables every morning.
